About

Leading the Way in Global Trade

Navya Group started its first venture as Navya International Trading FZCO, established in 2022 with coal trading, steel trading & commodity trading as its core business activities. Within a short span of time the company has achieved great success in coal trading. In 2026, the company started a fully owned subsidiary in Hong Kong.

As a global player, we have offices in:

  • United Arab Emirates (Dubai)
  • Hong Kong
  • India (New Delhi)

Why Choose Navya International Trading?

At Navya International Trading FZCO, we specialize in providing reliable and efficient energy solutions tailored to the coal and mining industry. Our solutions are designed to support coal mining, processing, handling, and industrial operations with dependable energy supply and improved operational efficiency. We focus on helping businesses optimize energy costs, maintain smooth operations, and meet the demanding requirements of the coal sector through innovative and sustainable energy solutions.
