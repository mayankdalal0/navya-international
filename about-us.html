About Us

About

Leading the Way in Global Trade

The Company was incorporated under laws of United Arab Emirates and registered in Dubai, with aim to establish itself as a prominent commodity company in space of energy. The company is involved in coal trading activities. The company as trader exports Indonesian non-cooking coal to markets such as Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Dubai & China. It has entered into coal offtake agreements with reputed miners in different regions of Indonesia. The company has traded over 2.3 million tonnes of coal in its last 3 years of operation and has planned to sale 2.0 million tonnes of coal in current year and double its capacity by end of 2028. Considering the market trend in global commodities market, it aims to achieve turnover of USD 50 million in current year 2026. The Company aims to export coal to new emerging markets like India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, New East, South and South East Asian markets.

As a global player, we have offices in:

  • United Arab Emirates (Dubai)
  • Hong Kong
  • India (New Delhi)
